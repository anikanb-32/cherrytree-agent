Cofounder Agreement

Company  ·  Executed July 13, 2026
Preamble

This Cofounder Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of July 13, 2026, by and between the undersigned founders (collectively, the "Cofounders") of Company, a Delaware C-Corporation (the "Company"). The Cofounders desire to set forth their mutual understandings and agreements regarding their roles, responsibilities, equity ownership, and other matters relating to the Company.

Section 1

Formation & Purpose

The Company was formed to —. The Cofounders agree to work diligently and in good faith toward the Company's mission and to act in the best interests of the Company at all times.

The Company's principal place of business shall be determined by the Cofounders. The Company's legal structure is a Delaware C-Corporation, with the intention of raising venture capital financing.

Section 2

Cofounder Information & Roles

The Cofounders and their respective titles are as set forth in Exhibit A. Each Cofounder agrees to devote their full professional time and attention to the Company, unless otherwise agreed in writing by all Cofounders.

Section 3

Equity Allocation

The Cofounders' initial equity ownership in the Company shall be as determined by the Cherrytree Equity Calculator and agreed upon by all Cofounders, as set forth in Exhibit B. All equity is subject to the vesting schedule described in Section 4.

Section 4

Vesting Schedule

All equity held by Cofounders shall vest over a period of 4 years, with a 1-year cliff. Upon the cliff date, 25% of each Cofounder's equity shall vest. The remaining equity shall vest monthly in equal installments over the following 36 months.

In the event a Cofounder ceases to be active with the Company before the cliff, they shall forfeit all unvested equity. After the cliff, unvested equity shall be subject to the Company's repurchase right at the lower of cost or fair market value.

Section 5

Decision-Making

Ordinary business decisions shall be made by majority vote of the Cofounders. Matters materially affecting the Company's direction, including but not limited to equity issuance, raising financing, sale of the Company, or amendment of this Agreement, shall require unanimous written consent of all Cofounders.

Section 6

Intellectual Property & Ownership

Each Cofounder hereby assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in any and all intellectual property created, developed, or contributed in connection with the Company's business, whether before or after the date of this Agreement. This includes inventions, software, trade secrets, designs, and any other proprietary materials.

Each Cofounder represents that they are not subject to any agreement that would conflict with their obligations under this Section.

Section 7

Compensation

Cofounder salaries, if any, shall be determined by mutual agreement and subject to the Company's financial capacity. Until otherwise agreed in writing, Cofounders shall defer compensation or accept minimal compensation as the Company requires.

Section 8

Performance & Accountability

Cofounders agree to set and review mutual performance expectations on a regular basis. In the event a Cofounder fails to perform their agreed responsibilities after reasonable notice and opportunity to cure, the remaining Cofounders may, by unanimous vote, initiate a buyout or separation process as described in the Company's bylaws.

Section 9

Non-Competition & Non-Solicitation

During the term of this Agreement and for a period of 12 months following departure, each Cofounder agrees not to engage in any business activity that directly competes with the Company, and not to solicit the Company's employees, contractors, or customers.

Section 10

General Provisions

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of law principles. Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall first be submitted to mediation, and if unresolved, to binding arbitration in accordance with the rules of JAMS.

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement among the Cofounders with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements. This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by all Cofounders.
